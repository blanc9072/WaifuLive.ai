good questions
Is she always watching my screen?

No. She can't see your screen until you switch that on, and you can turn it back off whenever you want. When it's off, she just doesn't see it.

Where does my data go?

Your voice gets transcribed right on your own computer. The chat itself, and anything she reads off your screen, runs through Google's Gemini, and your conversations get saved so she can remember you next time. We don't sell your data or use it to advertise to you.

Isn't an AI companion kind of a lot?

She's honest about being an AI, she only does what you've allowed, and she's meant to make your day a little better, not eat it up or stand in for real people. You decide how present she is.

How much will it cost?

Still figuring that out. If you're on the waitlist, you'll hear about pricing first and get in before everyone else.

What do I need to run it?

It's an app you download and run on your own computer, not a website you log into. She sits on your screen while you get on with everything else.
